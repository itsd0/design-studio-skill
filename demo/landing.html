Local-first file transfer

Your files cross the room, not the internet.

Ferry pairs two of your own machines and streams files directly between them — over Wi-Fi, Ethernet, or a cable. No accounts, no uploads, nothing stored in the middle.

macOS · Linux · Windows · no account required

What it does

Direct, resumable, and quiet.

Nothing in the middle

Files stream device to device. When Ferry says done, the bytes never left your network.

Resumes anywhere

Pause, close the lid, unplug the drive. The next run picks up at the byte it stopped on, not the file.

Pairs once, remembers

Two devices exchange one short code and stay bonded until you revoke them.

Terminal native

One command. Both ends.

Scriptable by default

Pipe stdin, target a device by name, or queue a folder for later. Every action in the menu bar has a command behind it.

ferry send ferry receive ferry watch
